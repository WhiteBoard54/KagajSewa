How pricing works

The prices shown are starting prices.

The final price depends on your company structure, the number of shareholders and which documents you need. We quote exactly before you order.

Government fees are separate

KagajSewa’s fee covers document preparation only. Fees, fines and taxes payable to the Office of the Company Registrar, the Inland Revenue Office, your ward office or any other authority are not included. You pay those directly to the office concerned.

Payment

By eSewa, Khalti or bank transfer, after you have seen the documents and are happy with them. There is no online payment on this website yet.

Contact

Tell us what you need. We will quote you today.

Phone, Viber, WhatsApp or email — whichever is easiest. Send your company name and what you are filing, and you will get a price and a turnaround time straight back.
